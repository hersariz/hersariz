## 👋 About me

I'm a frontend developer from Semarang, Indonesia. I spent the last year building production apps for international clients at **Nextera Tech** (Singapore, remote): an **Angular 19 data-visualization dashboard for a government client**, and **React/Next.js products for the Singapore market** — maps, payments, booking, and real-time features.

> 💼 Most of my professional work lives in **private company repositories**, so this profile only shows personal and side projects. My commercial experience is covered on my [portfolio](https://hersa-web.vercel.app).
